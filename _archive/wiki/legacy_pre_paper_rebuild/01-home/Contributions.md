# Contributions

Purpose: summarize what this project contributes in its current frozen version.

## Main contributions (Phase 1)

- A reproducible DTN scenario corpus (`corpus_v1`) with 60 scenarios across 7 families.
- A transparent analysis pipeline from `.settings` to reportable descriptor spaces.
- Dual-space evaluation (`core23` and `full46`) with explicit ablation.
- Public, traceable freeze narrative: improved baseline, stable and publishable, with declared limitations.

## Why this is useful

- Enables reproducible comparisons across routing protocols.
- Reduces ad-hoc scenario selection bias.
- Provides a clear bridge from corpus design to later benchmark and selector phases.
