# Dropped scenarios and evolution

Purpose: record scenarios moved out of the active benchmark set and why.

## Rationale

Scenarios are moved out of the active corpus when they do not improve benchmark value, typically due to high redundancy or overlapping narrative role.

## Current dropped set

The historical dropped set is kept in `corpus_dropped_v1/` for traceability.

Main reasons:

- high pairwise similarity with active scenarios,
- duplicated narrative role in the same family/regime,
- low marginal contribution to diversity goals.

## Policy

Dropped scenarios are not deleted from project history; they remain documented as part of corpus evolution.
