# Extended features

Purpose: define the role of descriptors outside core23.

## Why extended exists

Extended descriptors are kept for:

- richer diagnostics,
- family-specific interpretation,
- dashboard and supplementary analyses.

They are not the primary methodological baseline for final diversity reporting.

## Typical exclusion reasons from core

- derived/redundant with stronger descriptors,
- low coverage or highly conditional applicability,
- implementation detail with weak cross-family impact.
