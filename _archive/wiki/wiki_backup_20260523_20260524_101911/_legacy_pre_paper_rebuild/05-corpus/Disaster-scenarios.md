## Disaster scenarios (9)

This page summarizes the **Disaster** family scenarios and links to the full per-scenario documentation pages (EN). These scenarios cover complementary DTN regimes under disaster conditions: shelter hotspots, partitioned cities with mule bridges, erratic aftershock mobility, medical triage, UAV mules, short-TTL critical messaging, traffic storms, infrastructure recovery, and extreme 1-min TTL.
