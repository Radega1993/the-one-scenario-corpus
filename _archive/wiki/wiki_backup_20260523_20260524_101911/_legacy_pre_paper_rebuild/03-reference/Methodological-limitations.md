# Methodological limitations

Purpose: state residual limitations in the frozen baseline.

## Declared limitations

1. Some highly correlated scenario pairs remain, although materially reduced.
2. `core23` does not recover its earlier silhouette level after optimization rounds.
3. A strong residual dependency remains between `mm_WDM` and `mm_Bus` (`0.9393`).

## Positioning

These limitations are explicitly documented to support transparent, publishable methodology.
