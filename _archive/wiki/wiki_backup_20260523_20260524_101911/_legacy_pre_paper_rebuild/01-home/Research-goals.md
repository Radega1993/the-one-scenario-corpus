# Research goals

Purpose: define the scientific problem, scope, and expected value of Phase 1.

Related artifacts: `corpus_v1`, `analysis/run_analysis.py`, `analysis/reports/RESULTADOS_ACTUALES.md`.

## Problem

Routing evaluations in DTN/OppNets are often run on scenario sets with hidden redundancy, making results less robust and less transferable.

## Goal of this project

- Build a reproducible scenario corpus with broad coverage and controlled redundancy.
- Quantify structural diversity from scenario descriptors, not only narrative labels.
- Produce a documented baseline that can be reused for protocol benchmarking and later adaptive-selection phases.

## Phase 1 output

- Frozen, publishable baseline: 60 scenarios, 7 families.
- Official final metrics documented in [Final-frozen-results](Final-frozen-results).
- Declared limitations documented in [Methodological-limitations](Methodological-limitations).
