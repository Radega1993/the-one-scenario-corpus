# Research goal

**Status:** stable | **Updated:** 2026-05-20 11:41 UTC

## Purpose

Define the scientific question the corpus supports.

## Content

**Primary question:** How do opportunistic routing protocols behave under controlled diversity of mobility, connectivity, and traffic load?

**Secondary goals:**
1. Provide a reproducible scenario set (not a single map + RWP).
2. Separate **mobility**, **traffic profile (TP)**, and **map** levers.
3. Enable fair protocol comparison with documented limitations.

**Non-goals:**
- Emulating a specific real city deployment.
- Claiming empirical realism of contact traces.
