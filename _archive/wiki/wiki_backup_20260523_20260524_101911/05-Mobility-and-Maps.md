## Purpose

## Content

## Classification

| Component | Type | Justification | Methodological implication |
|-----------|------|---------------|---------------------------|
| Road/home/office WKT | Map-constrained / real geometry | OSM-derived paths | Spatial constraints are realistic |
| Movement models (WDM, RWP, …) | Synthetic / semi-synthetic | The ONE generators | Contacts are model-driven |
| Message Events | Synthetic | MessageEventGenerator | Load is designed, not measured |
| Contact times | Simulated | ConnectivityONEReport | Not Cabrero-style empirical |
| Deliveries | Protocol-dependent simulated | MessageStatsReport | Outcomes depend on router |

**Terms:**
- *Realistic* = plausible under model assumptions
- *Synthetic* = generated by simulator rules
- *Empirically observed* = **not claimed** for this corpus

## Maps

- **HelsinkiMedium:** majority of map-based bases
- **Manhattan:** U2, U4 (activity WKT projected from Helsinki — see limitations)
- **Campus/rural:** often abstract `worldSize` without OSM
